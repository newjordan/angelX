# Village and power progression captures

These images are generated by the ignored `write_powered_village_review` test in `crates/delve/tests/render/village__shots.rs`, using the actual `Run` simulation and shared renderer at 1152 × 672. The scenario starts a seeded settlement with normal founding supplies, selects plans through ordinary keyboard-shaped and pointer-shaped inputs, waits for production and deliveries, and builds the structures shown. It does not inject resource stock, completed buildings or ore claims.

The construction sequence establishes forestry, farming, stone and plank production, clay extraction, fired bricks, a cottage, walls and a gate. Cottage captures show separate foundation and half-wall stages. The test asserts that housing and food grow the population beyond the six starting residents, then serializes and reloads the full settlement snapshot.

The power sequence purchases the masonry charter, produces stone blocks, builds a riverbank waterwheel and roads, lays a northern shaft route, and installs a sawmill drive upgrade through the same input adapter. It verifies powered allocation, removes a shaft segment, checks that plank output stops for 30 simulation seconds, repairs the paid connection, and checks that production resumes. A second snapshot round-trip preserves the powered settlement.

These are actual gameplay progression captures from a scripted scenario, not a full player expedition. Ore encounter admission, guardian defeat and claim settlement have their separate evidence in the [encounter index](../encounters/README.md). The [source art review](../../../../cockpit/assets/kingdom/village/review/README.md) separately documents generated masters, animation alignment and render-only fixtures.

The retained captures were refreshed during the model-switch wind-down on 2026-11-05. `walking.png` includes the 744-pixel logical world viewport and continuous camera; `waterwheel.png` includes clear shaft corridors. Root inspected both images. `cut-shaft.png` and `restored.png` show the selected sawmill's actual power allocation. `capture.json` records the scenario facts and image checksums. The hero's frontier stride/facing update and native desktop mouse dispatch remain pending; these still images do not claim to verify either.
