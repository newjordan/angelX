# Kingdom Works image evidence

These checked-in images are reviewed captures from the shared game renderer.
Their sources and limits are recorded here so an art fixture is not mistaken
for a completed gameplay session. See [Kingdom Works](../../KINGDOM-WORKS.md)
for controls, implemented systems and test commands.

## Integrated factory screens

Generated by `write_frontier_review` in
`crates/delve/tests/render/kingdom_frontier__shots.rs`, at 768 × 448 pixels.
The scenario spends founding supplies through the real construction, research
and production commands, waits for local production and carrier deliveries,
and issues the produced Oathblade. Its iron and crystal claims are explicitly
injected visual-review encounter fixtures; these screenshots do not establish
that their guardians were defeated during this scenario.

`foundation.png`, `production.png`, `library.png` and `frontier.png` retain

`crates/delve/tests/render/claim_bosses__shots.rs`. The warning captures run the
real combat simulation and shared renderer, with review health refills so the
desired attack state can be observed. They demonstrate poses and telegraphs,
not encounter balance or survival with an untouched health bar.

The victory capture comes from a separate scripted guardian scenario using
normal attacks without resetting health or assigning a corpse. It demonstrates
real damage and death settlement; it is not a recording of a whole expedition
from the founding camp. Dedicated admission and progression tests cover those
separate transitions.

## Isolated art fixtures and source masters

[ore-poses.png](ore-poses.png) is an isolated renderer fixture. It compiles the
actual ore sprite renderer with synthetic rest/tell states and checks both
facing directions, silhouette changes, body scale and bottom anchors. It does
not run the encounter simulation or establish attack timing.

[The building contact sheet](../../../cockpit/assets/kingdom/buildings/review-contact.png)
uses the actual `Img` drawing helper to compare source-derived structures and
terrain at small sizes. It has no kingdom simulation state.

The [village construction and animation review](../../../cockpit/assets/kingdom/village/review/README.md)
uses the actual anchored sprite compiler and painter in a standalone fixture.
It covers cottage/wall/gate construction, connected wall shapes, timber bridges,
four distinct active machinery phases and all eight poses for four citizen
roles. Its two-second loop demonstrates rendering and animation, not economic
production or citizen task completion.

The full-resolution generated paintings remain in
[`cockpit/assets/kingdom`](../../../cockpit/assets/kingdom). Source manifests
retain prompts, selected source provenance, crop rectangles and checksums.
Build-time cropped/resized RGBA banks are runtime derivatives, not additional
generated masters. Source visual reviews establish complete silhouettes and
material readability; gameplay behavior is defined by the rules and card files.
