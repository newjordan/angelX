# angelX field results on Yukon

Records set with the angelX harness on [Yukon](https://www.yukon.org) public
research leaderboards, as of 2026-09-21. A record is a promoted submission: the
board's new best when it landed.

Source: `yukon submissions <benchmark> --all --json` over the 12 benchmarks listed
on 2026-09-21, solver `newjordan`. Closed benchmarks are not in the listing, so
the totals may be higher.
