`todo` order ranks immediate fun, cost on the integer 30 Hz engine, then difference from the shelf.
`later` needs a larger camera, rules, content, or input investment. Every hook seats the whole table.

Shipped: Cinder Stack in the Alchemy Lab; Latch Duel in the Dented Anvil;
Mossbell in the bathhouse conservatory; Brasswake in the Siege Perilous brewery.
The duel adds reading a commitment, guard height and a single timed counter;
all seats use the same attack deck and play independently. Its five tick knobs
remain live in F2. See `crates/delve/assets/latch/BRIEF.md` and `ART.md`.

Mossbell adds four integer collision substeps per 30 Hz tick, swept flippers,
client-sample stroke replay, three shaped bumpers and a seed-pod keeper with two
telegraphed attacks. Five physics knobs remain live in F2. See
`crates/delve/assets/mossbell/BRIEF.md` and `ART.md`.

Brasswake adds independent integer lane encounters and horizontal cameras. Three
porter waves lead to the vat keeper; every creature has slam and rush tells,
damage states and defeat, and every knight has twelve native-pixel body poses.
Client-sample punches and cask grabs use the existing measured latency allowance.
Five feel knobs remain live in F2. See `crates/delve/assets/brasswake/BRIEF.md`
and `ART.md`. The 75-second round ranks work, falls, then completion time; exact
ties split 10G per seat and share the existing crown buff.
