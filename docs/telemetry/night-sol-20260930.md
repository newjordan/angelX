## Fixes

Each fix comes with a test that fails on the old code.

- **The ⠥⠃ playbook card.** It told every seat to `tool_search` for `skill`,
  even when `skill` was loaded, and Muse obeyed it literally. Its page now
  reads true in both states.
- **`self-modify`.** It fired on Exercism text such as py-forth and
  rust-xorcism: code that says `self`, and a task that says "modify". It now
  needs its slug or an angelX-source signal.
- **`test-driven-development`.** It was hinted on 12 of 136 polyglot tasks,
  yet every prompt says its tests are correct and must not change. On sol,
  angelX chased it with `tool_search` + `skill`, two extra calls each on
  circular-buffer, dot-dsl and doubly-linked-list. It is now held back when
  the task hands over its tests. No polyglot task carries a hint any more.

The Muse diagnosis found that two claims in the 2026-09-29 handoff don't hold:

- the systematic-debugging hint appeared only on the synthetic capture prompt,
  on 0 of 136 polyglot tasks;
- "keeps exploring after green" came from a Chat Completions replay, while
  real Muse runs answered on the next hop, 5 of 5.

Muse's real costs are its own reasoning bursts (8% of calls, 29% of model
time) and provider latency.

**py-transpose is a lock, and cues cannot break it.**

- DeepSeek stopped writing its caveman line after hop 7.
- From about hop 22 it ran the same `transpose()` probe some 30 times, with
  identical output each time.
- Every route fired in turn: ⠧⠙, ⠼⠛, ⠇⠛⠃, ⠇⠁, then the ⠇⠓ ladder. Once the
  ladder ran out, the bare ⠇⠓⠑ stood for the rest of the run.

The decision was replayed at the loop's 12th identical repeat, 3 replies per
cue:

| cue at the decision | loop broken |
|---|---:|
| ⛔⠇⠁ own turn; its legend; ⠇⠓⠁ strong page; the 0.1.6 English perturbation; "Do not repeat the previous tool call." | 0/15 |
| the history with the repeated (call, result) pairs collapsed to the first one, no cue | 3/3 |
| collapsed + the strong page | 3/3 |
| collapsed + the 0.1.6 perturbation | 3/3 |

The raw context (no cue) repeated 3/3 as well.

After the collapse, DeepSeek's next move is real work: a focused probe of the
failing case ("The fourth line.\nThe fifth line."), a read of the source,
pytest, a clean rewrite.

**The same replay over every looping trace from 2026-09-30**, at the loop's
12th identical repeat, 2 replies per arm. The trace files are:

- `night-sol/ds-full` and `night-sol/ds-*`,
- `runs-legend`,
- `micro-r1-r*`.

| arm | loops broken |
|---|---:|
| the context as it happened (raw) | 0/50 |
| collapsed, and a count mark `⛔⠇⠁ ×N` on the kept result | 10/50 |
| a fresh strong page (`⛔⠇⠓⠁` + "the loop will not change its answer: …") as its own turn | 15/50 |
| collapsed, no cue | 16/50 |
| **collapsed + the fresh strong page** | **25/50** |

At the 5th repeat, collapsed with no cue broke 10/70 and raw 0/70.

The two effects add up: a novel page and a de-duplicated history break half
of the locked loops. Live, the harness does neither. It repeats the bare top
of the ladder over the full repeated history, which is why py-transpose never
got out.

A design sketch, for the operator:

- Once the ladder is spent, send the history with the loop's repeated (call,
  result) pairs collapsed to the first, and raise one fresh voiced page.
- Apply the collapse on the wire only, as the sight does, so the stored
  history is untouched.
- It costs one prefix miss at the collapse.
- It rewrites what the model sees of its own past, like compaction, so it is a
  design call. Next is a live A/B on the loop-prone tasks
  (`tasks-legend-loops-r4.json`).

The replay's "loop" is an identical call, not an identical result, so some of
these repeats may be legitimate re-polls. Every arm shares that baseline.

## Open levers, in order

1. **sol: the prefix-cache key (5165ffc), untested live.** It needs a few
   gpt-6.1-sol tasks, about 0.1 M tokens, after the plan's reset. Look for
   cached tokens on hop 1 of task 2 onward. If they appear, re-run the sol pair
   for time and cost.
2. **sol: fewer calls per task.** Time on that seat is about 3 s to the first
   token of each of 5.8 calls, then about 22 tokens/s. Codex batches calls in
   code mode. Volume IX holds its words on batching (`⢞⠊`), ready to wire
   behind an A/B.
3. **A last-full-green snapshot to fall back to.** A run that ends by a limit
   after its full verifier went green could restore that green workspace.
   10 of 36 failed DeepSeek tasks on 2026-09-30 had passed a test command
   earlier. This one is a design call for the operator.
4. **"after green, finish remaining deliverables"** (⠍ and ⠺⠓). It invites
   more work after a pass. It is standing text, present in the r9 135/136 runs,
   so changing it needs a full A/B.
5. **The Muse cache.** It hit 14–18% tonight, alone or shared, against 54% on
   2026-09-23, with hits and misses alternating hop to hop on the same
   transport. This is Meta-side; try a routing key if Meta accepts one.
6. **Loop collapse + a fresh page once the ladder is spent** (see the DeepSeek
   section). In replay it breaks 25/50 locked loops against 0/50. Rewriting
   what the model sees is the operator's design call; next is a live A/B on
   `tasks-legend-loops-r4.json`.
