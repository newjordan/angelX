## What shipped

**Chariot racing — the realm's Rock n' Roll Racing.**
`cockpit/src/drive/together_shooter/chariot.rs` (new). Hold F at the
Hippodrome coach plate (`hippodrome::COACH`) to board in the stable's
best herd horse; an empty yard runs a keep nag, unrecorded. Four
chariots, three laps of a 1200-pace oval: A/D steers, Space whips (wind
is the stamina economy), E drops caltrops (3/race), corners cap pace by
cornering blood and lane, shouldering in corners is settled by nerve,
crashes cost 15 ticks and shake the screen. Announcer is state:
`Chariot::call()` ("LET THE CARNAGE BEGIN!"). Purse 90/45/20 by place,
settled from `chariot:{place}:{horse}` marks in
`app/control/dungeon_realm.rs` beside `settle_stable`. Pure in inputs +
seed like the joust; mirrored in `mirror.rs` (field, snapshot, apply);
the driver is reined via `joust::reins` while racing.

**Horses: lore from usage, full market.**
`cockpit/src/drive/chivalry.rs`: `Bloodline` (pace/stamina/corner/
nerve, deterministic per model-house key), `Horse` (house, sire/dam,
blood, starts/wins/seconds/earned), and on `Stable`: `herd`,
`yards` (calls served per house), `foals`. `note_service` is wired
live in `app/control/turn_io.rs::note_horse_service`, called from
`fold_turn_cache` (exactly once per finished turn): the lead house
takes the turn's reported model-call delta (minimum 1), each seated
formation house takes 1; saved via `realm.save()`. Lore is derived,
never stored beside the record (`Horse::lore`). Playable commands in
`app/control/dungeon_chivalry.rs`: `/dungeon stable market`,
`buy <n>`, `sell <n>` (7/10 of value), `breed <a> <b>` (mean blood +
one mutation, yard capped at 12), all settled clone→save→
`rebuild_home` with full rollback on save failure; `status` recites
every herd horse's lore. Market stock is pure in the yards record, so
the listing read is the listing sold.

## Boundaries and traps for the next hand

- The working tree carries ~70 files of the operator's own uncommitted
  work (risen, second grimoire, mummers, etc.). It was preserved
  byte-for-byte outside the hunks above. My edits inside shared files
  are small and grep-able: `chariot` in `together_shooter.rs` (mod,
  Run field, init, Hippodrome branch in `advance`),
  `mirror.rs` (3 lines), `dungeon_realm.rs` (9 lines),
  `turn_io.rs` (one call + one method). Do not `git checkout` shared
  files to "clean up".
- `rustfmt` from this toolchain reformats beyond the repo's pinned
  style (repo-wide `fmt --check` already fails on hundreds of files).
  One arena file was churned and restored to HEAD style with hunks
  re-applied by hand; format only new files, in the file's own style.
- There are now **two** chariot games and that is deliberate: the comic
  fixed-point pastime (`pastimes/chariot.rs`, cabbages, on foot) and
  this hippodrome sport (real herd horses, gold, records). Do not merge
  them; `docs/CHARIOT.md` states the split.
- `Stable` derives `Hash`; `Horse`/`Bloodline` derive it too — the herd
  fields broke that first. Old saves load because every new field is
  `#[serde(default)]`, and a malformed stable still resets alone
  (existing lenient test still passes).
- Settlement trusts mark names with spaces as underscores
  (`chariot:1:Bolt_watch`). Market-generated names are single tokens;
  a future name source with underscores will corrupt the parse.
