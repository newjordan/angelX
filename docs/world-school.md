# Sloptomizer School of Magic

The school stands beside the market gardens, west of the Artisan Quarter.
Two elves and two wizards live there. Visit without interrupting the knight:

| Command | Location |
| --- | --- |
| `/world visit school` | Twin towers and the school grounds |
| `/world visit school-study` | The study and its comparison instrument |
| `/world visit school-vault` | An archive one level underground |
| `/world follow` | Return the camera to the working knight |

The existing Enter control also opens the study while viewing the grounds.
Leave returns from either room to the school grounds.

The study's instrument lights while contextual evidence collection is active.
The archive holds paired passing and failing receipts for the same check.
Its readout shows `OBS` (retained observations), `CHECK` (distinct check
identities), `+/-` (checks with both pass and fail receipts), and `?`
(inconclusive receipts). These are evidence counts; formal Sloptomizer
experiments still own fitness measurements. Empty shelves stay empty until
actual receipts arrive.

These are pixel-world locations, with explicit study and underground views.
Visiting, entering, and leaving affect only the camera. The school creates no
model calls, training jobs, research limits, or progression rewards. Reduced
motion and motion-off preferences hold in both rooms.
