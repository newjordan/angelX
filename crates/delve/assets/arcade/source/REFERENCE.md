# Retained reference prompts

The PNG references are original concept sheets. The knight concept is rejected
historical material, retained for comparison only. Its native replacement is
hand-authored pixel rows in `scripts/arcade-knight.py`; no knight image is opened,
traced or sampled by the builder. Effects retain their source extraction and
palette mapping in `scripts/arcade-art.py`, with native core, smoke and debris
additions in `scripts/arcade-fx-polish.py`.
No commercial frame or character was used as an input.

## Knight

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite animation reference sheet. One ORIGINAL squat weathered knight, 1990s Neo Geo hand-drawn arcade run-and-gun principles, NOT any existing franchise character. Flat neutral grey background, no floor, shadows, text, captions, gridlines, labels or particles. Exactly 6 columns by 5 rows, 30 evenly spaced cells with generous empty margins. Each sprite consistently same scale, about 46 native pixels tall; present at enlarged crisp pixel-art grain. Big rounded dented blue-grey kettle bascinet with raised brow and TWO expressive warm amber eyes in a wide dark visor, asymmetrical bent coral-red horsehair plume curling backward, broad layered shoulder plates, patched burgundy short surcoat, fat leather gloves, broad heavy boots, small battered round shield with offset brass boss on left hand, stubby broad sword in right hand. Original medieval salvage knight, no modern military costume. Thick unbroken warm indigo/brown contour, internal overlaps inked, 5-7 hue-shifted tone steps in steel and cloth, broad cel-shaded clusters, warm bounce, modest glints, visible leather strap, rivets as short strokes not dots. Dark desaturated colors, NO white, NO dithering, NO noise, NO smooth/vector gradients. Face readable. Design at 46px, not tiny detail.
All poses three-quarter facing RIGHT. Rows read left to right:
Row1: 4 different breathing idle poses (plume, torso, shield breathe), then 2 running poses contact and passing.
Row2: 4 more running poses (airborne stride, opposite contact, passing, airborne) then 2 jumping poses (crouch lift and upward stretch).
Row3: falling with raised plume, squash landing, attack anticipation sword drawn back and knees bent, slash start, slash extension, slash followthrough.
Row4: hit recoil one, hit recoil two bent, guard shield forward one, guard braced two, defeat stunned knees buckle, defeat kneeling.
Row5: defeat tipping over, defeat collapsed comic-gritty heap (no gore), victory fist raise one, victory fist raise two, victory settled proud, extra neutral back view.
Keep weapons and limbs wholly inside each cell. CONSISTENT character, rich rounded painted-then-inked forms, lively exaggerated poses, dark-room-friendly saturation.

## Effects

Use case: stylized-concept. Production pixel animation effects sheet, original 1990s hand-painted then inked arcade cartoon language. Exactly SIX columns by FIVE rows, evenly spaced cells on flat neutral mid grey background, NO words, captions, grids, shadows or floor. Every effect entirely inside its cell, ample margins. Limited hue-shifted cel ramps, thick warm indigo/brown contours, bold connected clusters, NO dithering, speckles, noise, near-white or smooth gradients. Dark desaturated violet smoke, warm brick orange lobes and restrained amber cores, brightest core muted honey below RGB 225,200,145. Big expressive outlined smoke billows and radial HOT flash shapes with fireball lobes. Debris as short chunky 2-4px linear strokes not floating pin dots.
Cells 1-12 row-major: one explosion animated from tiny compact amber flash, expanding orange fire lobes, large lobed fireball, hot amber hollow core inside rust orange lobes, maximum layered explosion, fire recedes under smoke caps, rising smoke billows with orange underlighting, smoke expands/curls, smoke dissipation, smaller soot curl, thin smoke curl, last smoke wisp. Coherent evolving explosion, not twelve random static effects.
Cells 13-15: 3 muzzle flash frames pointed to RIGHT, compact onset / long lobed flame / short receding.
Cells 16-19: 4 impact star frames, squat bold orange burst / broad irregular impact star / dark outlined fading star / smoky curl.
Cells 20-24: 5 dust puff frames, brown violet outlined floor-hugging lobes from small compressed / expanding / wide / breaking / last curl.
Cells 25-27: 3 hit sparks, gold/orange rightward crossed chunky linear strokes, compact / extended / broken curling.
Cells 28-30: extra smoke, ember fragment and dust reference.
At the intended native grain effects around 28-48 px wide; make clean broad shapes that survive downsampling, no white-hot core or generic vector clipart.
