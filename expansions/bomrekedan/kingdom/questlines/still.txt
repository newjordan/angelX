# The Still Runs Dry — Zuglar Lessonshoot, brewer, the stills behind The Copper Peppers.
# A Bomrekedan questline (quests.json: the-still-runs-dry). Same file shape
# and caveats as steel.txt: `power bomrekedan:` needs a line in quest_gate();
# cued steps count by standing with the teacher; `needs` steps wait on a
# counter nothing counts yet.
power   bomrekedan:still
teacher Zuglar Lessonshoot
place   the eight stills behind The Copper Peppers, on the Hearth level of Bomrekedan
room    Tavern
look    a leather cap stained purple to the brim, a dipping ladle on a cord, and the slow smile of a dwarf tasting
line    Plump helmet wine again. It is good wine. It is the only wine.
teach   A still is honest. Put fruit in and it gives you drink. Put nothing in and it gives you a brewer with nothing to do.
hook    A slate by the stills is ruled into columns of 'no fruit', and the columns run off the slate and down the wall.
step    The tasting | taste this year's plump helmet wine from the cask Zuglar holds out | still_tastings 1 | Good, isn't it. Eighteen hundred barrels of good, and not one of anything else. | cue taste
step    The fruit basket | gather fruit and leaves in the Greenwood and carry them home | fruit_gathered 6 | Apples. Cherries. Something red. Anything that was never a mushroom. | needs
step    The barrels | carry three barrels from the carpenters to the stills for Zuglar | barrels_carried 3 | A brew needs somewhere to go. The brewers put it down a thousand times for want of a barrel. | cue deliver
step    The tavern's tale | buy a round at The Copper Peppers and hear who drank it | tavern_tales_heard 1 | Bards stay longer when there is something new in the cup. So do erinyes. | cue tale
trial   The toasted hall | clear one hall on a delve you began with a drink from the taverns of Bomrekedan | toasted_halls 1 | There. Now it is a story, and the story needs a drink.
reward  the Stills raised a level: both taverns pour every night (a Bomrekedan questline: no power is taught)
title   the Fruit-Bringer
keepsake a dipping ladle on a cord, still smelling of cherries
ties    The tavern's tale pools with Sela's quarrel at Maud's (tavern_tales_heard); the barrels are Oddom Gearedsecrets' (Barrels and Pots); the fruit is the Orchard lot's.
