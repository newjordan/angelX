# The Erinys at the Gate — Eigriteil Rainsuitors, the erinys mayor, at Bomrekedan's drawbridge.
# A Bomrekedan questline (quests.json: the-erinys-mayor and
# the-erinys-at-the-gate, joined). Same file shape and caveats as steel.txt.
# Sir Castor also teaches in the Gate room; give the two different `at`.
power   bomrekedan:erinys
teacher Eigriteil Rainsuitors
place   the drawbridge of Bomrekedan, where seven erinys warriors once waited and were turned away
room    Gate
look    grey wings folded tight, a poet's slate under one arm, and a mayor's chain worn as if it were lent
line    Two hundred and forty-nine years, and the dwarves have made me their mayor. I am still deciding how I feel.
teach   A petition ignored is not forgotten. The asker remembers it, and so does whoever was standing next to them.
hook    Seven names are cut into the gate's left post, small, at the height of an erinys's hand: every warrior who asked to live here in the first year and was ignored.
step    The seven names | read the seven names on the gatepost aloud with the mayor | petitions_read 1 | Gorafio, Sketefio, Giful, Kerafio, Efitarjeggin, Gurefio, Elmul. Say them so they are said. | cue read
step    The two codices | read Elements of the Erinys and Uncovering the Erinys at her side, one each visit | codices_read 2 | Dwarves wrote about us. Erinyes wrote about us. Only one of them was right, and it was not always ours. | cue read
step    The mayor's hours | sit three petitions with the mayor at her office | offices_sat 3 | Now you have heard what a mayor hears. Mostly it is about beds. | cue sit
step    The oath | see four erinys residents sworn as citizens of the Kingdom of Meat Gods | erinys_sworn 4 | Residents for years, citizens now. Gurefio wept, and denies it. | needs
step    The winged company | take an erinys companion down into the Delve and bring them home standing | erinys_companions_home 1 | Home, and standing. That is the whole of the promise. | needs
trial   The winged hall | clear one hall with an erinys companion beside you and neither of you falling | erinys_unbroken_halls 1 | Seven were turned away. You have brought one home. Six to go.
reward  the erinyes join the delve roster as companions (a Bomrekedan questline: no power is taught)
title   the Gate-Opener
keepsake a grey feather, tied with the mayor's red cord
ties    Sir Castor keeps the Delve's own gate (gate_sieges_held), and Ast Ringtwinkled keeps this one against the ratfolk (the ratfolk line); the codices are on the library's shelves, with The Struggle Understood the naga want back.
