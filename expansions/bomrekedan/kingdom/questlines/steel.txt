# The Steel Line — Fath Quietinked, metalsmith, the Metalsmith's Forge of Bomrekedan.
# A Bomrekedan questline (quests.json: the-steel-line). Written in the shape
# of cockpit/assets/dungeon/questlines/*.txt and parsed by the same rules
# (crates/delve/src/rules/questline.rs), but not placed there yet.
# `power bomrekedan:<id>` is a new kind: quest_gate() knows wish:, skill: and
# kingdom: only, so until it learns `bomrekedan` (open to every knight, like
# kingdom:) the teacher speaks only her idle line. `kingdom:` is not usable:
# it may count only the campaign's vec_ counters.
# Cued steps are counted by standing with the teacher (the only way the realm
# counts a step today). Steps flagged `needs` wait on a counter nothing
# counts yet. `at` is left for whoever places the teacher.
power   bomrekedan:steel
teacher Fath Quietinked
place   the Metalsmith's Forge on Bomrekedan's deep forge level, below the mine-head
room    MineHead
look    a soot-black apron, a plain iron scimitar at the hip, and burn scars counted up both forearms
line    One smelter, one forge, eleven smiths. You can see the trouble from here.
teach   Steel is four things married: pig iron, iron, flux and fire. Miss one and there is no wedding.
hook    The tally slate over the forge reads 1,394 in chalk, one mark for every job put down for want of a bar.
step    The ore tithe | bring Fath three ore from the Mines | ore_given_to_forge 3 | Hematite, limonite, magnetite. Any of them, so long as it is ore. | cue forge
step    The coke watch | stand the coke watch at the one smelter while it burns the coal | coke_watches_stood 1 | Coal first, then ore, then steel, all in the one furnace. That is why it took years. | cue stand
step    The flux | bring dolomite flux home from the Mines | flux_carried 10 | Ten stones of dolomite. The smelter itself is built of it, so it knows the taste. | needs
step    The pig iron | stand with Fath while the pig iron pours | pig_iron_pours 1 | Pig iron is half the bar. The other half is patience. | cue pour
step    The kit | bring a full kit's worth of ore for one recruit's breastplate, greaves, helm, gauntlets and boots | ore_given_to_forge 8 | Three hundred breastplates were put down for want of this. Not this one. | cue forge
trial   The steel recruit | clear one hall wearing every piece of mail the delve's start gave you, none of it broken | steel_kit_halls 1 | It held. Write it on the slate, and rub one mark out.
reward  the Forge raised a level: mail more each delve (a Bomrekedan questline: no power is taught)
title   the Steel-Married
keepsake a stamp of the forge's mark, pressed in red wax
ties    The ore tithe pools with Master Wymond's and Underforeman Doorne's (ore_given_to_forge); Kikrost Metalcloisters burns the charcoal (Coke for the Smelter); the six steel squads wear what comes out.
