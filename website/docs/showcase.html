The showcase

angelX opens in its own window on a desktop (--term keeps it in the terminal). There, whatever the agent presents opens full size in a floating panel above the terminal. The mini-viz keeps its preview as before; the showcase is the same media let out of the pane. In a plain terminal nothing changes.

What opens it

  • The agent's present tool (images, video, reports, data).

  • /show <path>, and Scryglass's Open (o) on the selected media.

  • A chart the graph tool finishes.

  • /hide closes it.

Who brings it in

A squire of the serving house kicks the folded panel open, then sits on its roof by the close button, legs over the edge, and rides along when it is dragged. He wears his house's colours (the field on plume and tabard, the charge on his emblem); with no house serving he is the Keep's own. While a formation is seated, up to three squires of its other houses follow him in and take seats beside him. Under the pointer a squire raises a hand and names himself: his house's first knight, his castle and his epithet. Seated, they face the pointer, stand when it goes to the close button, wave when new media arrives and swing their legs now and then. On close they walk the panel off.

Charts are brought by the wizard, the pixel cousin of the intro's dotted wizard: he raises his staff, a spark crosses to the panel, it unfolds from its middle and the chart is swept in under a drift of motes. Over a bar or point he points his staff at it and its tip shows the value. Idle, he lifts his staff now and then; on close the panel folds into a flash.

The panel

It is glass: a hairline frame with rounded corners over a faint dim, the terminal just showing through, and no shadow. Charts, models and pages carry no backing of their own; they stand on the glass.

  • Drag the title bar to move it; a double-click fills the window.

  • The corner grip sizes it.

  • The arrows at the left of the bar step back and forth through the last thirty-two showings.

  • The copy button copies what is showing: a picture as an image (as its path where the system has no image clipboard), a page or data file as its text, a graph chart as its data (CSV), a video or model as its path.

  • The close button, or Escape while the pointer is over the panel. Anywhere else Escape stays the cockpit's (it interrupts a running turn).

Only the newest showing reaches the panel: a slow one (a big model) asked for before a quick one gives way to it.

Code

  • crates/glass/src/showcase.rs: the panel. The window owns it: its frame, controls and pointer handling, in window pixels on the window's thread.

  • crates/glass/src/squire.rs, crates/glass/src/wizard.rs: the heralds, drawn as text and timed as functions of time since open or close.

  • cockpit/src/ui/showcase.rs: what is shown, made off the draw thread (pictures, video, pages, models), its history, the houses' colours and names.

  • cockpit/src/ui/showcase_chart.rs, cockpit/src/ui/showcase_model.rs: charts and smart graphs; OBJ and GLB.

  • Tests sit beside each in their own modules.
