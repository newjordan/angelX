# Tourney asset inventory

- `plates/`: arena, arrival, crowd and ceremony stills.
- `poses/`: hero, challenger, herald, maiden and noble-lady cutouts.
- `fx/`: pennants and shield-crack overlays.
- `sequences/`: numbered animation frames, per-sequence `loop_meta.json`, and
  [index.json](sequences/index.json).

These are the installed terminal assets. The renderer loads frame sequences
from their checked-in metadata; playback timing is controlled by the cockpit.
