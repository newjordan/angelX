# The phrasebook: wishes the Delve knows by heart.
#
# A known wish lands at once, in the game as it runs. Granted again, it
# climbs its ladder: the first grant is modest, the last is the full gift.
# Anyone can add one; the card checker bounds every effect line.
#
#   wish <id> | <Name>
#   kind <hold | spell | guard>          (hold when left out)
#   say  <a way a player puts it> | <another> ...
#   tier <card lines, ; between> :: <what the scroll says>
#   draw <slot> [at=<place>] [from tier]
#   <rows of inks, frames side by side with | between>
#
# Effects are the card words (see /dungeon cards new): volley, bounce,
# homing, chain, mend, pierce, shots, damage, rate, speed, armor, vamp,
# max_hp, orbit, burst; spells add cast, heal, ward, nova and summon.
# Sprites (worn, shot, orbit, summon, aura) are how a wish shows in play.
