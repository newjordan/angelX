# The phrasebook: wishes the Delve knows by heart.
#
# A known wish lands at once, in the game as it runs. Granted again, it
# climbs its ladder: the first grant is modest, the last is the full gift.
# Anyone can add one; the card checker bounds every effect line.
#
#   wish <id> | <Name>
#   say  <a way a player puts it> | <another> ...
#   tier <card effect lines, ; between> :: <what the scroll says>
#
# Effects are the card words (see /dungeon cards new): volley, bounce,
# homing, chain, mend, pierce, shots, damage, rate, speed, armor, vamp,
# max_hp.

wish volley | Triple Volley
say triple wide fire my shots | triple shot | fire three | three wide | spread my shots | spread shot | shotgun | wide shots | triple fire
tier volley 5 :: Every 5th shot flies three wide.
tier volley 3 :: Every 3rd shot flies three wide.
tier volley 1 :: Every shot flies three wide.

wish ricochet | Ricochet
say bounce my shots | shots bounce | bouncing shots | ricochet | bank shots | bounce off walls
tier bounce 1 :: Shots bounce off a wall once.
tier bounce 2 :: Shots bounce off walls twice.
tier bounce 3 :: Shots bounce off walls three times.

tier homing 2 :: Shots curve hard toward monsters.
tier homing 3 :: Shots hunt the nearest monster.
