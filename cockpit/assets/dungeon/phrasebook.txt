# The phrasebook: wishes the Delve knows by heart.
#
# A known wish lands at once, in the game as it runs. Granted again, it
# climbs its ladder, up to five tiers: the first grant is modest, the last
# is the full gift. The fourth and fifth are the deep's: a ladder climbs
# to its fourth from the second floor down and its fifth from Dragon
# Keep's. Where the third already gives what wishes give, the fourth and
# fifth add a word beside it. Anyone can add one; the card checker bounds
# every effect line.
#
#   wish <id> | <Name>
#   kind <hold | spell | guard>          (hold when left out)
#   say  <a way a player puts it> | <another> ...
#   tier <card lines, ; between> :: <what the scroll says>
#   draw <slot> [at=<place>] [paint=<sha256> [normal=<sha256>]] [move=<words>] [from tier]
#   <rows of inks, frames side by side with | between>
#   (or sheet <slot> <file.png> ..., or paint <slot> <file.png> ...)
#
# Effects are the card words (see /dungeon cards new): volley, bounce,
# homing, chain, mend, pierce, shots, damage, rate, speed, armor, vamp,
# max_hp, orbit, burst, fork, fart, and the literal ones a wish asks for
# by name: size (bigger shots), knock, burn, chill, poison, boomerang and
# velocity; spells add cast, heal, ward, nova and summon.
# Sprites (worn, shot, orbit, summon, aura) are how a wish shows in play.
