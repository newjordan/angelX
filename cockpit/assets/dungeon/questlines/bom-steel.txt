# The Steel Line — Fath Quietinked, metalsmith, the Metalsmith's Forge of Bomrekedan.
# A Bomrekedan questline, placed from expansions/bomrekedan/kingdom/questlines/
# steel.txt (quests.json: the-steel-line) and adapted to the engine:
# `power bomrekedan:<id>` teaches no power and opens to every knight
# (quest_gate). Every step but one is cued (standing with the teacher counts
# it, once a visit), and so is the trial (`cue` in its fifth field). The one
# counted step waits on the works ledger (`bom_built_<work>`, noted at the
# sign of a built work). The counters are this line's own: none is shared
# with a power's questline, so nothing walked here moves a skill's level.
# She stands on the Forges (`room BomForges`), on the floor east of the
# Metalsmith's Forge (kingdom.json workshop-metalsmithsforge-1391).
power   bomrekedan:steel
teacher Fath Quietinked
place   the Metalsmith's Forge on the Forges, Bomrekedan's deepest kept level
room    BomForges
at      51 18
look    a soot-black apron, a plain iron scimitar at the hip, and burn scars counted up both forearms
line    One smelter, one forge, eleven smiths. You can see the trouble from here.
teach   Steel is four things married: pig iron, iron, flux and fire. Miss one and there is no wedding.
hook    The tally slate over the forge reads 1,394 in chalk, one mark for every job put down for want of a bar.
step    The ore tithe | bring Fath a load of ore from the stockpiles, one each visit | forge_ore_tithed 3 | Hematite, limonite, magnetite. Any of them, so long as it is ore. | cue forge
step    The coke watch | stand the coke watch at the one smelter while it burns the coal | coke_watches_stood 1 | Coal first, then ore, then steel, all in the one furnace. That is why it took years. | cue stand
step    The flux | carry the dolomite flux from the stone stockpile to the smelter at her side | flux_carried 1 | Ten stones of dolomite. The smelter itself is built of it, so it knows the taste. | cue carry
step    The pig iron | stand with Fath while the pig iron pours | pig_iron_pours 1 | Pig iron is half the bar. The other half is patience. | cue pour
step    The kit | lay out a full kit's worth of ore for one recruit's breastplate, greaves, helm, gauntlets and boots | kit_ore_given 1 | Three hundred breastplates were put down for want of this. Not this one. | cue forge
step    The steel line | see the steel line raised in Bomrekedan, ovens to bar stock | bom_built_steel_line 1 | A line, not a forge. Now the slate can start going down.
trial   The steel recruit | stand with Fath while the first recruit is fitted in steel off the line, breastplate to boots | steel_recruits_fitted 1 | It held. Write it on the slate, and rub one mark out. | cue fit
reward  the Forge raised a level: mail more each delve (a Bomrekedan questline: no power is taught)
title   the Steel-Married
keepsake a stamp of the forge's mark, pressed in red wax
ties    The ore tithe is the fort's own, not Master Wymond's or Underforeman Doorne's; Kikrost Metalcloisters burns the charcoal (Coke for the Smelter); the six steel squads wear what comes out.
